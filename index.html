01 / The introduction

A little about me.

I'm Brendan Wilson, a 2015 Algonquin College Graphic Design graduate with over a decade of experience across branding, advertising, motion graphics and commercial print production.

Since graduating with honours, I've worked in agency environments, developed visual identities for businesses, produced multimedia campaigns and spent years mastering the technical side of bringing designs into the physical world.

I've always believed great design is about more than making something look good. It's about understanding the message, solving problems and creating work that connects with people.

Returning to Algonquin would bring my journey full circle, allowing me to contribute the knowledge, creativity, and practical experience I've gained over the years to the college that helped me get started.

BRENDAN WILSON / COVER LETTER

A full-circle opportunity.

Re: Part-time Graphic Designer, Marketing Department (R177270)

Dear Hiring Committee,

Some opportunities feel like a natural next step. Others feel like coming full circle. The opportunity to return to Algonquin College as a Graphic Designer is both.

I graduated from Algonquin's Graphic Design program in 2015 with honours, finishing at the top of my class and receiving the Bruce Rawlings Award for Excellence. My time at Algonquin shaped the designer I became, and the possibility of bringing a decade of professional experience back to the college where my career began is genuinely exciting.

My career has spanned graphic design, motion graphics, brand development, digital advertising and commercial print production. At McMillan Advertising, where I was hired following my co-op placement, I developed motion graphics, video content and creative assets for clients including QuickBooks and United Rentals. Working in a collaborative agency environment strengthened my ability to interpret briefs, contribute creative ideas and deliver polished work under deadlines.

As an independent designer, I've led projects from concept through execution. For Enviro-Stewards, I developed a complete visual identity, supporting assets and brand guidelines. For Ener-C, I created digital advertising campaigns involving creative direction, filming, editing, compositing and motion graphics. These experiences strengthened my ability to translate communication objectives into effective visual solutions.

My extensive background in commercial print production gives me a practical understanding of how designs move from screen to finished product. Combined with proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign and After Effects, as well as experience in Cinema 4D and Blender, I bring a versatile skill set across print, digital and multimedia applications.

What makes this opportunity especially meaningful is the chance to contribute to an institution that had such a positive impact on my own life. I know firsthand what Algonquin can mean to its students, and I would take genuine pride in creating work that supports the college's identity, communicates its opportunities and connects with future students.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience and connection to Algonquin College could contribute to your Marketing team.

Sincerely,
Brendan Wilson

07 / How I work

Creative tools evolve. The responsibility to communicate clearly doesn't.

01

Understand the audience

Start with the people, the purpose and the problem. Good design should make communication easier.

02

Explore intelligently

Sketch, prototype and test. Use digital and AI-assisted tools where they add value, with human judgment driving the decisions.

03

Deliver in the real world

Design for the final medium, from mobile screens to physical production. The work isn't finished until it works.
